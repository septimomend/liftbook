Liftbook privacy policy

Liftbook is a personal workout tracker that runs entirely in your browser.

  • Data on your device. Your training plan, logged sets, settings and pictures are stored in your browser's storage on your device.
  • Google Drive. If you sign in with Google, Liftbook uses the drive.file permission. With it the app can only see and change files it created itself, which is a Liftbook folder in your own Google Drive. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • No servers, no tracking. Liftbook has no backend. It does not collect, sell or share any data, and it does not use analytics or advertising. Your data goes only between your device and your own Google Drive.
  • Removing your data. Clear the site's data in your browser, delete the Liftbook folder in Google Drive, and remove Liftbook's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
